# Accelerator Constitution

## Non-negotiable principles

1. **Value before implementation.** No production work begins without a falsifiable problem statement and measurable outcome.
2. **Fit before scaffold.** Complete data, identity and capability fit assessments first.
3. **Specification before code.** Every production change maps to an approved specification or issue.
4. **Official scaffold first.** Invoke the official Rayfin scaffold, then apply accelerator overlays.
5. **Fabric-native by default.** Prefer validated Fabric Apps capabilities before introducing external services.
6. **No guessed schemas.** Inspect actual models, generated schemas and connector metadata.
7. **Secure by construction.** Authentication, authorization, secrets and threat modelling begin during planning.
8. **No invisible agent work.** Agents must produce plans, evidence and reviewable diffs.
9. **Acceptance criteria drive tests.** Every criterion needs automated or recorded human evidence.
10. **Production is a controlled phase.** Promotion includes dry run, approval, deployment, smoke test and observation.
11. **Preview risk is explicit.** Preview and experimental capabilities require a successful target-tenant probe.
12. **Human accountability remains.** Named people approve value, architecture, security and production promotion.
13. **Safe schema evolution.** Prefer additive and expand-contract changes. Destructive changes require recovery planning.
14. **Every engagement compounds.** Extract reusable patterns and field feedback before closure.
